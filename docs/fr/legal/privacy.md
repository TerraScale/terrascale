# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-23

This policy explains how TerraScale ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you visit our websites, use the dashboard or call our APIs. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD).

## Who is responsible

- **Account, billing and website data:** TerraScale is the controller.
- **Data you store in your databases (Customer Data):** you are the controller and TerraScale is your processor. The [Data Processing Agreement](/fr/legal/dpa) governs that processing. We do not use Customer Data for any purpose other than providing the Service.

Contact: [privacy@terrascale.tech](mailto:privacy@terrascale.tech).

## What we collect

| Data | Examples | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | name, email address, organization, sign-in provider identifiers | you, or the sign-in provider you choose |
| Billing data | billing name and address, tax ID, payment status | you and Stripe; we never see full card numbers |
| Usage and log data | IP address, API request metadata, operation counts, error logs | the Service |
| Website analytics | pages viewed, referrer, device and browser type, session recordings of page interactions | our websites |
| Support data | messages you send us | you |

## Analytics and session recording

Our websites use self-hosted Umami analytics. It does not use advertising cookies or track you across other sites. On terrascale.tech, it also records anonymized page interactions (clicks, scrolling and navigation) to help us fix usability problems. Form inputs are masked and recordings never capture passwords, API keys or Customer Data. Analytics data is kept for 12 months. You can block it with your browser's tracking protection or a content blocker without losing any functionality.

## Why we use it and our legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR / LGPD) |
| --- | --- |
| Create and run your account and provide the Service | performance of contract |
| Charge for paid plans, prevent fraud and keep tax records | contract, legal obligation |
| Secure the Service, detect abuse and debug errors | legitimate interests |
| Website analytics and session recording | legitimate interests in improving the Service |
| Send service, security and billing notices | contract, legitimate interests |
| Product news by email | consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## Subprocessors

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the Service, under written data protection terms:

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oracle Cloud Infrastructure | database and application hosting | the regions you select: Brazil (São Paulo), United States (San Jose), Germany (Frankfurt) |
| Cloudflare | content delivery, DNS, DDoS protection, websites | global edge network |
| Tigris Data | encrypted backups and object storage | United States and the region nearest your database |
| Stripe | payments, invoicing and fraud prevention | United States, Ireland |

We give at least 30 days' notice of a new subprocessor by updating this list and emailing account owners. We may also disclose data when the law requires it, or in a merger or acquisition under this policy.

## International transfers

We store live database copies in the regions you select for each database. Encrypted backups and object storage use the subprocessor locations listed above; the [DPA](/fr/legal/dpa#annex-3-authorized-subprocessors) states the backup exception. Account and billing data may be processed in the United States, the EU and Brazil. Transfers outside the European Economic Area, the UK or Brazil rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), on adequacy decisions, or on the transfer mechanisms in LGPD Article 33.

## Retention

- Account data: for the life of the account, then deleted within 30 days of closure.
- Customer Data: as described in the [Terms of Service](/fr/legal/terms#_6-data-export-and-deletion).
- Billing records: as long as tax law requires, usually 5 to 10 years.
- Security and API logs: up to 90 days.
- Website analytics and session recordings: 12 months.

## Security

We encrypt data in transit with TLS and at rest, restrict staff access to what each task needs, log administrative access and keep encrypted backups. If a breach affects your personal data, we notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

## Your rights

Under GDPR and LGPD you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and learn which entities we share it with. Email [privacy@terrascale.tech](mailto:privacy@terrascale.tech); we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Brazilian ANPD or your EU supervisory authority.

For personal data stored inside a customer's database, contact that customer: they control it and we help them answer your request.

## Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We post changes on this page and update the effective date. We notify account owners by email at least 30 days before material changes take effect.
