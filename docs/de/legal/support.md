# Support availability

Contact support at [support@terrascale.tech](mailto:support@terrascale.tech) or through the [dashboard](https://dash.terrascale.tech/). Each plan has a first-response target:

| Plan | Response target |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Community |
| Starter | 2 business days |
| Pro | 1 business day |
| Team | 8 hours |
| Enterprise | Custom, arranged through sales |

These are response targets, not a service-level agreement (SLA). The same targets appear in [plan entitlements](/de/limits#plan-entitlements). Keep diagnostics free of credentials and customer data.

See [authentication](/de/guides/authentication) and [service availability](/de/getting-started/).
