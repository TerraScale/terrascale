## Status vocabulary and evidence rules

Use one status for every ledger ID and every SQLite scoreboard case:

- **NOT VERIFIED**: no authoritative evidence yet.
- **PASS**: all stated checks pass at a cited source revision, supported configuration, and reproducible artifact set.
- **FAIL**: an executed check contradicts the requirement; preserve the failing evidence and a remediation link.
- **OPEN**: a required case is unsupported, inconclusive, missing, or not comparable; state the reason and next evidence needed. `OPEN` is not completion.
- **NEEDS DECISION**: a required input or behavior is unspecified by the goal/current source; record the concrete decision and its rationale before dependent work can be accepted.
- **N/A (approved scope)**: only for a genuinely inapplicable item with a documented scope decision and retained prior evidence. Optional future HDD criteria remain deferred until that tier is proposed; they do not block initial SSD+S3 qualification.

Every PASS must cite: TerraBase source revision; benchmark-repository revision when applicable; configuration and feature/platform set; exact command or run-manifest ID; immutable report/raw-data path; and relevant test/benchmark names. A test name without a successful execution receipt is insufficient. A source link alone proves implementation presence, not behavior. A production-provider claim additionally requires provider-specific evidence. Do not convert a score average into per-case acceptance.

Choices the goal requires but does not set, including complete workload cases, supported durability profiles, coordination protocol, S3 providers, VM images and public query semantics, are tracked as `DEC-001..009` in the evidence ledger. Some cases and fixture pins are provisionally registered; the remaining decision scope and runtime evidence still need reconciliation. Until recorded, do not imply those choices were verified or score dependent claims as complete.

## Per-case SQLite scoreboard contract

An average, geometric mean, or marketing summary cannot close any individual case.
