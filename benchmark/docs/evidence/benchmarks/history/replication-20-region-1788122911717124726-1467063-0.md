# Twenty-Region replication benchmark

status: diagnostic

Source candidate: `dba15ad16e24 (dirty)`
Timestamp (Unix nanoseconds): `1788122911717043031`

This lane runs 20 Regions with two real durable composed Members per Region on one host. It injects a fixed synthetic GRU-origin RTT floor for `GlobalDurable`. The run exercises durable engine and CRDT application, acknowledgement checks, loss, retry, and convergence. It is not real WAN evidence and cannot support a production or competitor speed claim.

Configuration: 3 samples, 10 operations per sample, 2 warm-up operations, RF=2, 40 Members, 256-byte payload, concurrency 1.
