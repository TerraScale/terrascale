# PITR proof gate G0b at 1M documents: LOCAL S3 EMULATOR — NOT TIGRIS

**Pre-fence harness, provisional.** Every run here used a `pitr-bench` built before the harness rework (phase fences
on every transaction, clock-free cut quiesce, reconcile after restart). These results are recovery and reproduction
evidence only. Fenced acceptance of G0b needs fresh writes with the reworked CLI (a new harness agent reruns the core
matrix). The bucket is PitrLab's disk-backed S3 emulator (`s3-serve`) on loopback, so nothing here says anything about
Tigris uplink, latency or object semantics. Plan: [pitr-100m.md](../docs/pitr-100m.md).

## Defects

### Product (TerraBase, owners: backup)

1. **Log shipping stops for minutes after every new generation, and `/v1/backup` hides it.** After a restart the
   database's change log is not read until the post-snapshot restore drill finishes. Meanwhile `/v1/backup` reports
   `lagSeconds` 0, `pendingBytes` 0, `logBytes` 0 and `lastError` null, and `terrabase_backup_lag_seconds` is 0.
   - G2 (after SIGKILL): drill done 17:33:14 (`drills_total` 6→7), first log read 17:33:16 (a 196 MB backlog at once),
     5 min 10 s after the restart, with writes running the whole time.
   - G1 (graceful restart): 0 segments in 3 min 22 s until the kill.
   - Evidence: `source/evidence/samples-source.jsonl` 17:24:41–17:33:16Z, `out/audit-src-db.json`,
     `out/ck-status-before-kill.json`.
   - Repro: start the pinned binary with a bucket, write continuously, restart, then poll `/v1/backup` and the bucket's
     `wal/<new generation>/`.
   - At 1M the backlog stayed under 256 MiB and backup then covered every acknowledged write (C5 exact on Paths A and
     B). How long the drill blocks at larger sizes is unmeasured.
2. **Restore memory.** A 1M-document point-in-time restore (S2 254 MB + 548 MB of log) peaked at 7.4 GB RSS on the
   source node. Measurement only; G2 must measure the trend.
3. **A generation's final segment has no end marker.** Deleting it is invisible to the bucket audit (`ffinal0`,
   `ffinal2`: `ok`). A restore at a time after it would end at the previous segment. This is the plan's known risk; it is
   unconfirmed here because no fault restore ran.

### Harness

- `pitr-bench cut` (`internal/pitrbench/ops.go`, `TakeCut`) fails at once with "the node does not list database … in
  /v1/backup" when the database is newer than the backup's 10 s discovery (`ContinuousBackup.cs:58`). It should wait
  within `--wait`. Proposed patch: treat `!ok` like "not shipped yet" until the deadline.
- `StartNode` passed an inherited `flock` descriptor to the detached node, so `/tmp/ts-heavy.lock` stayed held after its
  script exited (O3's node pid 595447, about 10 minutes). Fixed by O3; `pitr-run.sh` uses `flock -o`.
- The fence `curl` example from the lead sent `X-TerraBase-User-Id` on a data route and got 403 `forbidden_scope`.
  Data routes need no owner header (`pitrbench/client.go`).
- `pitr-run.sh` itself had two bugs: the empty-ledger ack count (the degenerate C1 above) and in-place edits of a
  running bash script (a harmless syntax error at the end of one step). Later edits replace the file atomically.

## Not verified

Fault-matrix restores (all cases, including the wrong master key), P3 (restart with immediate writes over a delayed
snapshot), T±1 ms cuts around u1/d1/r1, a non-degenerate bounded cut during load-a, and anything about Tigris.
Restarting the Path B target mid-run changed its GC settings, which does not change what a verify compares.
