# CI and image publishing baseline, 2026-10-23

The change separates PR Slow from required Fast and production publishing from checked build stages.
Targets: PR/merge-group `test` <=10 min, merged-to-published images <=8 min. Targets require real runs;
these baseline samples do not establish the result.

Baseline collection used one last-100 list per discovered workflow and five job-detail calls.
Each timed row below has **n=1**, so its sample median is that observation. These are distinct workloads,
not a representative median over 100 runs. Queue/startup is created-to-first-job; wall is first job
start-to-last job completion. Job-minutes sum active jobs without GitHub billing rounding.

The sampled merge-group TerraBase job spent 7:12 in checked build and 8:39 in Fast/replication/local E2E;
auth spent 4:15 and 8:21 respectively. Removing PR Slow alone cannot prove the ten-minute target.
The sampled main publishing jobs took 12:33 (auth) and 13:36 (TerraBase), including respectively 10:44
and 10:20 in the repeated checked-build/cache-export action. These are publishing-job durations,
not exact merge-to-registry intervals.

Current main has CI and weekly CodeQL workflow files. Previews and the separate image workflow were
already removed before this change; historical Preview failures do not justify recreating them.
File-based weekly CodeQL has no runs in the collected baseline; its behavior and security coverage remain.

Three real AFTER runs, exact required-check duration, publication timestamps, and live revision proof
will be recorded in the owning PR body. No runs are dispatched solely to expand the baseline.
