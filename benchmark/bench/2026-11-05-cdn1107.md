# Deleted shape CDN URLs: issue #1107

## Historical failure

[Acceptance issue #1107](https://github.com/TerraScale/terrascale/issues/1107) retains the original failure:
seven SDK-used ciphertext URLs returned 200/HIT in all three regions, 21/21 requests at 527.137–529.930 seconds after
successful database deletion. That historical failure remains valid. Epoch-key release was already 404 everywhere.

The database was Pro, with its home moved from gru to fra. Its successful DELETE completed at 18:33:44Z. GRU's service
log recorded `shape_copies_pending` at 18:34:20.251871Z and only reached `verification_pending` at
18:47:04.428674Z, about 800 seconds after deletion. The physical cleanup path scanned the whole shared shape bucket,
HEADing every object serially to compare owner metadata, with a 30-second visit budget and one-minute erasure cadence.
Other deletion jobs shared that worker. The R2 inventory sampled afterward contained 703 retained objects, similar to
the earlier F11 inventory (704). F11's `h1-000240d9abffea65` sorted near the start; acceptance's
`h1-f1f3db08a628b1dd` and `h1-f379f47f1be80432` sorted near the end. Its 72.153-second single-object
observation did not establish a bound for later-sorting objects and shared deletion jobs.

The delay happened before bounded CDN admission. Plan tier is not part of `ShapeObjectStore.Owner`, the URL validator,
or the purge request; the same path serves Pro and Free. Smart Tiered Cache was inspected and remained on. A diagnostic
`cf cache purge` of the original `h1-f1f3db08a628b1dd/` prefix was accepted at about 18:48Z and returned 404/BYPASS from
FRA afterward. This supports prefix purging for the exact URL form; it does not repair the historical 420-second result.
No new permission, credential, cache rule, setting or paid capacity was added. The teardown fixes remain intact.

## Correction

Before publishing any new or adopted object, the mirror writes a private ownership marker in the existing R2 bucket.
Successful DELETE joins every node's shape publishers, lists only its ownership prefix, admits exact URLs to the durable
outbox and deletes their R2 objects before 204. It removes inventory markers after deletion. Full admission returns a
pending deletion instead of losing work. The old shared scan remains for orphan recovery and objects never adopted by
the current service. Public URL forms and ciphertext encryption are unchanged.

The worker processes the oldest 100 of at most 2,000 admitted URLs, with an 18-second pass budget and two-second cadence.
The documented healthy-provider deadline is **420 seconds after successful DELETE**, in all three regions. It checks
R2 absence, the Cloudflare HTTP response and `success: true`, then GETs every exact public URL and requires 404 before
clearing a batch. Prefix purge includes Origin variants and every tier. Errors retain durable work, use retry backoff,
and surface through queue count, oldest age, per-attempt outcome and verification-failure metrics. Provider failures can
exceed the deadline; key destruction remains synchronous.

[Contract](../../docs/terrabase/crypto-shredding-f11.md#cdn-deadline-after-delete).
