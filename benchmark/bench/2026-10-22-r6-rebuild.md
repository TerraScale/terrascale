## Scope and correctness target

Baseline review: `e7fa366dd`; code includes ReplicaRebuild, RebuildCertification, RebuildRecovery, FaultAwareReplicaSource, ReplicaIdentityFile, ReplicaCopyFaults and connected engine/service callers. Paths below are relative to `src/TerraBase/`; original line numbers identify reviewed code, not necessarily current positions.

Recovery must produce the lattice join of validated source state and verified receiver-only state, not byte-for-byte source equality. Completion requires a fresh incarnation, FULL refill/barrier and certificate, no active refusal or pending ledger, retained-file deletion only after certification, and successful later replication. Client reads/writes must refuse uncertified replacements. Active-operation crash protection remains conservative; this change does not promise availability after arbitrary in-flight native failures.

## Safeguards reviewed

1. Source incarnations are pinned; requested buckets, unique ordered keys, decoded bounds and 4,096 keyed digests are checked, with at most eight retry rounds under the same pin.
2. Stored-state digests and envelope CRCs reject ordinary corruption before repair; replication data/ACK reads check refusal before and after. This is not Byzantine-source protection.
3. FULL refill/salvage uses bounded page memory and verifies retained-state digests. Reads use a working copy; the retained original remains until certification.
4. Durable ledger plus FULL tenant barrier controls certification; foreign-member proofs are withdrawn. Publication, crash, disposal and restart boundaries are covered by the cited tests. These statements combine code review and cited coverage, not an unqualified durability guarantee.
