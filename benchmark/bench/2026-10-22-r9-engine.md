`_tb_merkle` scan back to back on the same fixture, host and process. Each size has
one warm-up per query and five measured repeats. Every measured output equals the

validate integer-valued corruption in a cached summary; rebuild the copy in that
case. State, bucket-page and salvage paths retain keyed-state validation.

Beta: AMD Ryzen 5 PRO 6650H, 6 cores / 12 logical CPUs, approximately 14 GiB RAM,
Linux 7.3.0, SDK `11.0.100-rc.1.26425.128`. During A, sampled one-minute load ranged
21.37–27.78 and CPU pressure `some avg10` 18.24–48.22%; during B, load ranged
29.62–35.01 and pressure 43.06–54.73%. The shared host was busier during B.

This run shows lower write throughput and higher CPU/allocation costs for B;
it provides no whole-engine speedup claim and does not isolate the maintenance
cost from the change in host contention. The query improvement trades repeated
full scans for transactional summary maintenance. No durability settings changed.
