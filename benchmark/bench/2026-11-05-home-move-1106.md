`01M6PENV06CRPSJYDDRZTF75R4`, moved GRU → FRA while SJC acknowledged local
writes. The customer reconciled 1,928 expected IDs against 1,926 distinct rows.

## Root cause

Local commits publish `TBP1` encrypted envelopes to an origin stream, with the
home and placement epoch observed at transaction start encoded in subject and
headers. Normal delivery decrypts before decoding. The promotion drain instead
called the raw LZ4/JSON decoder on ciphertext and silently skipped each envelope.
Only the former home's filtered consumer received those subjects; after fencing,
it could not apply them. The new home's normal consumer filtered out those
subjects, and its finite background scan could not handle arbitrarily late
old-placement commits either.

Lease observations preserve all-regions placement with an empty explicit replica
list. HTTP/WebSocket SQL admission and the local commit path checked that list
without honoring all-regions mode, causing SJC to redirect to FRA and the SJC pin
to fail at FRA.
