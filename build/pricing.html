Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our pricing and plans.

A Database Operation is any single read, write, update, or delete action against your database. Each query you execute — whether fetching a row, inserting a record, running an aggregate, or replicating to another region — counts as one or more operations depending on complexity.
All plans support overage billing at usage rates ($1 per additional GB of storage, $1 per million additional operations). You'll never be cut off mid-request — we'll notify you well before you approach limits so you can upgrade or adjust.
Anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. No lock-in, no cancellation fees.
Yes — your data is yours. Full exports are available anytime through the dashboard or API, and we never charge a cent to get it out. The only constraint is a per-request rate limit so a runaway export job can't destabilize the cluster for other tenants; there's no cap on total volume and no exit fee. If TerraScale isn't working for you, you leave with everything you put in.
Yes. For teams with specific compliance requirements, custom SLAs, or dedicated infrastructure, our Enterprise plan is fully tailored. Contact sales to scope a deployment that fits your needs.
