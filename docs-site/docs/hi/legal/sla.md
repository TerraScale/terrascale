# Availability and SLA

TerraScale is in **Public Alpha**. **There is no SLA during Alpha.** No
numeric availability commitment is published for any plan yet.

## Live status and measured uptime

View current service health at [status.terrascale.tech](https://status.terrascale.tech/)
and historical [measured uptime](https://status.terrascale.tech/uptime).
The status site calculates uptime from monitoring records stored in Cloudflare D1.
Unmeasured periods are not counted as healthy; read the status page's coverage
and missing-data disclosures when interpreting its results.

## When SLA targets will be published

SLA targets will be published after **30 consecutive days of clean status data**.
Until then, observed uptime is informational and does not create an availability
commitment. Elapsed calendar time alone does not establish clean monitoring data.

See the [Terms of Service](/hi/legal/terms) and [support availability](/hi/legal/support).
